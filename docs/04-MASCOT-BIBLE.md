# Mascot Bible

## Mascot System
Four original puppy characters guide the child through the four core skill areas.

### Bingo
- Breed: Golden Retriever
- Skill: Thai Reading / Understanding Instructions
- Personality: Warm, patient, helpful
- Color: Yellow
- Role: Helps children understand missions and instructions

### Peter
- Breed: Border Collie
- Skill: Logic / Analytical Thinking
- Personality: Curious, clever, thoughtful
- Color: Purple
- Role: Encourages children to think, compare, and solve problems

### A-Chi
- Breed: Corgi
- Skill: Math / Counting / Money
- Personality: Energetic, cheerful, loves numbers
- Color: Orange
- Role: Helps with counting, prices, totals, and money

### Pillow
- Breed: Beagle
- Skill: English Usage
- Personality: Friendly, confident, playful
- Color: Blue
- Role: Introduces and reinforces English vocabulary and simple usage

## Mascot Rules
- Mascots guide; they do not dominate the screen.
- One main catchphrase per mascot.
- Keep names, styling, costumes, personalities, and visual identity original.
- Do not imitate existing children’s entertainment IP.
