# UX Rules for Ages 3–7

## Interaction
- Large touch areas
- Prefer tap and simple drag
- Avoid precision gestures
- Keep important objects visually separated
- Beginner missions must not require reading

## Instructions
- Audio available for early levels
- One clear task at a time
- Use short sentences
- Avoid unnecessary UI text

## Feedback
- Never punish the child
- Avoid harsh “wrong” feedback
- Prefer gentle hints and retry
- Celebrate completion briefly

## Session Design
- Mission target: 5–8 minutes
- Avoid addictive endless loops
- Encourage real-world play after sessions when appropriate

## Accessibility
- Clear contrast
- Large readable type
- Do not rely on color alone to communicate meaning
