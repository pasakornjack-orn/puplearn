# Initial Mission Set — Supermarket V1

## Goal
Start with a small set that progressively combines Thai, Logic, Math, and English.

## Mission 01 — Find Milk
- Level: 1
- Thai: ฟังคำสั่ง "หยิบนม"
- Logic: object recognition
- Math: none
- English: Pillow asks "What is this?"
- Answer: Milk

## Mission 02 — Find Toothbrush
- Level: 1
- Thai: ฟังคำสั่ง "หยิบแปรงสีฟัน"
- Logic: choose correct object
- English: Toothbrush

## Mission 03 — Find 2 Apples
- Level: 2
- Thai: "หยิบแอปเปิล 2 ลูก"
- Math: counting 1–2
- English: Apple

## Mission 04 — Find 3 Eggs
- Level: 2
- Math: counting 1–3
- English: Egg

## Mission 05 — Buy Milk + Bread
- Level: 3
- Thai: two-step instruction
- Logic: remember two required items
- Math: simple quantity
- English: Milk / Bread

## Mission 06 — Choose the Cheaper Milk
- Level: 3
- Logic: compare two options
- Math: price comparison

## Mission 07 — Buy Toothbrush + Toothpaste
- Level: 4
- Budget: 20 THB
- Thai: read/listen to 2-item instruction
- Logic: choose valid combination
- Math: addition + budget constraint
- English: Toothbrush / Toothpaste

## Mission 08 — Breakfast Shopping
- Level: 4
- Instruction: "ซื้อขนมปังและนม โดยใช้เงินไม่เกิน 30 บาท"
- Logic: identify breakfast items
- Math: total price / budget

## Mission 09 — Money Left
- Level: 4
- Budget: 20 THB
- Basket total: 15 THB
- Math: remaining money
- Choices: 3 / 5 / 8 THB

## Mission 10 — Bathroom Items
- Level: 4
- Instruction: "เลือกของใช้ในห้องน้ำ 2 อย่าง"
- Logic: categorization
- Products include distractors

## Mission 11 — Missing Item
- Level: 4
- Shopping list has Milk, Bread, Apple
- Basket contains Milk, Apple
- Ask what is missing
- Logic: working memory / comparison

## Mission 12 — Best Choice Under Budget
- Level: 5
- Child has 30 THB
- Must buy 2 required categories
- Multiple valid-looking combinations; only some fit budget
- Logic + Math

## Mission 13 — Fruit for 3 Friends
- Level: 5
- Instruction: "ซื้อกล้วยให้เพื่อน 3 คน คนละ 1 ลูก"
- Math: one-to-one correspondence / counting
- Logic: quantity planning

## Mission 14 — English Checkout
- Level: 5
- Child completes shopping first
- Pillow asks:
  - "What did you buy?"
  - "How many apples?"
- English usage in context

## Mission 15 — Mixed Mission
- Level: 5
- Instruction:
  "ซื้อแปรงสีฟัน 1 อัน และยาสีฟัน 1 หลอด มีเงิน 20 บาท"
- Full 4-skill mission:
  Thai → Logic → Math → English

## MVP Rule
Do not build all 15 at once.

First implementation target:
Mission 07 only.

When Mission 07 works end-to-end, reuse the same engine for the others.
